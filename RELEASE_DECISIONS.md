# Checks before the international App Store launch

The owner directed publication of the EN/ES site documents as version 1.0 on 2026-09-15. Publication does not settle every regional compliance question. Complete these checks before making Petfy broadly available in the listed markets or claiming that the app and website meet all local requirements:

1. **Controller identity — clarified 2026-09-15; European representative pending:** the site uses “Arvilo Labs” as the brand and identifies **Brandon Stevens Aragón Mejía** as the actual owner/controller in privacy and terms. Arvilo Labs is not described as a registered corporation. The public contact is the confirmed `arvilolabs@gmail.com` inbox; personal phone and postal address stay off this site. Because Petfy plans ongoing availability to people in the EU from Nicaragua, assess whether an EU representative must be designated under Article 27 and, if so, publish that representative's identity and contact details. The owner intends to review the result with Codex and does not plan an outside legal review.
2. **Children:** the owner sets no product-wide age minimum. The published copy tells families to obtain authorization where local law requires it and offers a deletion contact. Petfy has account data, precise foreground location, pet health notes, and an AI feature, so child-privacy duties and any parental-consent implementation still need market-by-market assessment. Do not claim the app has a parental-consent mechanism without verification. A general-audience product can acquire obligations when it has actual knowledge that it collects personal data from children under 13 in the United States.
3. **Legal bases and international transfers:** version 1.0 maps contract, AI consent, security/support legitimate interest, and legal purchase retention to the purposes described. Verify those bases against the live flows and document applicable transfer safeguards/provider terms, including Supabase in US East, OpenAI, Google Places, RevenueCat, Sentry, and push delivery, before a broad EEA launch.
4. **Retention:** version 1.0 states purpose-based criteria and the standard OpenAI abuse-log maximum. Determine operational backup/diagnostic/support/security/transaction periods and provider settings, then update the policy where the current criteria are insufficient for a territory or service change.
5. **Support inbox — owner confirmed 2026-09-15:** `arvilolabs@gmail.com` receives messages, and the owner will monitor it for support, privacy, and deletion requests. Routine delivery should still be tested as part of launch QA.
6. **App and site consistency:** version 1.0 has governing-law/consumer-rights language, effective date, and EN/ES copy. Review the in-app legal links, consent screen, App Privacy answers, and metadata URLs against this exact version before App Review. Additional locale-specific legal text needs separate review if introduced.
