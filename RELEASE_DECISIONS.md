# Decisions before making these documents effective

The site can be redesigned and reviewed locally now. These items prevent presenting the revised copy as a final legal policy or updating App Store Connect to point to it:

1. **Controller identity in Europe:** the owner wants only “Arvilo Labs” shown publicly, but says it is an unregistered brand. GDPR Article 13(1)(a) requires the identity and contact details of the controller. A qualified reviewer must decide how to comply without misrepresenting the brand; the current preference alone does not settle this.
2. **Children:** the owner sets no product-wide age minimum. Petfy has account data, precise foreground location, pet health notes, and an AI feature, so child-privacy and parental-consent obligations must be assessed by market. Do not claim a parental-consent mechanism exists in the app without verification.
3. **Legal bases and international transfers:** map processing purposes to lawful bases and document applicable safeguards/provider terms, including Supabase in US East, OpenAI, Google Places, RevenueCat, Sentry, and push delivery.
4. **Retention:** approve time periods or objective criteria for diagnostics, security/consent events, support messages, purchase records, and backups. The technical inventory has not fixed all provider periods.
5. **Support inbox:** send and receive a test message at `arvilolabs@gmail.com` and confirm it is monitored for support/privacy/deletion requests.
6. **Terms and release version:** approve governing-law/consumer-rights language, effective date, document version, and faithful EN/ES texts. Review the in-app legal links, App Privacy answers, and metadata URLs against the same version.

Primary sources: [GDPR Article 13](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679), [Apple account deletion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app), and [Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
