# Decisions before making these documents effective

The site can be redesigned and reviewed locally now. These items prevent presenting the revised copy as a final legal policy or updating App Store Connect to point to it:

1. **Controller identity and European representative:** the owner wants only “Arvilo Labs” shown publicly, but says it is an unregistered brand. GDPR Article 13(1)(a) requires the identity and contact details of the controller. We must resolve how to identify the actual controller without misrepresenting the brand before claiming an effective European privacy notice; the current preference alone does not settle this. Because Petfy plans ongoing availability to people in the EU from Nicaragua, assess whether an EU representative must be designated under Article 27 and, if so, publish that representative's identity and contact details. The owner intends to review the result with Codex and does not plan an outside legal review.
2. **Children:** the owner sets no product-wide age minimum. Petfy has account data, precise foreground location, pet health notes, and an AI feature, so child-privacy and parental-consent obligations must be assessed by market. Do not claim a parental-consent mechanism exists in the app without verification. A general-audience product can still acquire obligations when it has actual knowledge that it collects personal data from children under 13 in the United States.
3. **Legal bases and international transfers:** map processing purposes to lawful bases and document applicable safeguards/provider terms, including Supabase in US East, OpenAI, Google Places, RevenueCat, Sentry, and push delivery.
4. **Retention:** approve time periods or objective criteria for diagnostics, security/consent events, support messages, purchase records, and backups. The technical inventory has not fixed all provider periods.
5. **Support inbox — owner confirmed 2026-09-15:** `arvilolabs@gmail.com` receives messages, and the owner will monitor it for support, privacy, and deletion requests. Routine delivery should still be tested as part of launch QA.
6. **Terms and release version:** approve governing-law/consumer-rights language, effective date, document version, and faithful EN/ES texts. Review the in-app legal links, App Privacy answers, and metadata URLs against the same version.

Primary sources: [GDPR Articles 13 and 27](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679), [EDPB territorial-scope guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-32018-territorial-scope-gdpr-article-3-version_en), [FTC COPPA FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions), [Apple account deletion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app), and [Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
